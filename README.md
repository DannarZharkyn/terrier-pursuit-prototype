**Terrier Pursuit 🐾**

Terrier Pursuit is an interactive, web-based scavenger hunt platform developed for Boston University Student Wellbeing to make campus events more engaging, collaborative, and fun.

The platform allows organizers to create and manage events while participants explore campus, complete challenges, scan QR codes, and track their progress in real time.

**Key Features**

* Event Management: Create and customize scavenger hunt events.
* Team Participation: Join events, form teams, and collaborate.
* QR Code Integration: Scan QR codes to complete location-based challenges.
* Progress Tracking: Monitor participant and team progress.
* Organizer Dashboard: Manage events and monitor participation.

**Tech Stack**

* Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* Backend & Database: Supabase
* Deployment: Vercel

**Project Background**

I worked as the lead software engineer on Terrier Pursuit as part of the Boston University Student Wellbeing team, collaborating with colleagues to bring the platform to life.

The platform has been used by over 400 students across university orientation and community events and continues to evolve based on participant feedback and stakeholder needs.
